\noindent 3.2 Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria\par
\noindent Inclusion criteria. A paper enters the core system tables when it satisfies at least one of the\par
\noindent following:\par
\noindent  1. It describes a runtime diagnostic operation for foundation-model agents: failure localiza-\par
\noindent  tion, observability or tracing, safety intervention, or diagnostic benchmarking.\par
\noindent  2. It describes a serving-time resource management mechanism (memory, scheduling, cache,\par
\noindent  or power) reused by or designed for agentic workloads.\par
\noindent  3. It provides a baseline or architectural prerequisite directly reused by multi-agent diagnos-\par
\noindent  tic or serving systems.\par
\par\smallskip
\noindent Exclusion criteria. We exclude: (a) orchestration-only frameworks without runtime diagnos-\par
\noindent tic or serving mechanisms (e.g., workflow graph definitions); (b) single-model inference op-\par
\noindent timization without multi-agent or agentic extensions; (c) offline-only evaluation methods that\par
\noindent do not address runtime behavior; (d) long-term memory architectures without serving-time re-\par
\noindent source management; and (e) general agent surveys that do not provide system-level implemen-\par
\noindent tation details for diagnosis or serving.\par
\par\smallskip
\noindent Selection cutoff. The reference list was frozen for editorial inspection on 7 November 2026.\par
\noindent This is a revision cutoff, not a systematically reconstructed search date: the original exploratory\par
\noindent collection did not preserve database-by-database queries, retrieval timestamps, or screening\par
\noindent exclusions. We therefore describe the corpus as a curated landscape rather than claiming\par
\noindent PRISMA-style systematic completeness.\par
\par\smallskip
\noindent 3.3 Evidence Coding\par
\noindent Each included system is assigned an evidence tier that reflects the depth of our verification:\par
\noindent  • Tier 1a (T1a): Full-text reading with section-level evidence extraction. Quantitative\par
\noindent  claims, method details, cost metrics, and limitations are traced to specific sections, tables,\par
\noindent  or figures in the source paper. All systems in the core comparison tables are at least T1.\par
\noindent  • Tier 1b (T1b): Full-text reading but without page-level citation (e.g., the evidence was\par
\noindent  extracted from the complete paper but specific section numbers were not recorded).\par
\noindent  • Tier 2 (T2): Abstract-level verification. The system’s existence, claimed function, and\par
\noindent  headline results are confirmed, but detailed method mechanics and cost breakdowns are\par
\noindent  not independently verified.\par
\noindent  Abstract-only evidence is marked [a] in the text and is not used to compute cross-system\par
\noindent deltas or rankings. A source-level evidence-audit checklist accompanies the supplementary\par
\noindent material.\par
